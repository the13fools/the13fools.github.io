In 2026 I graduated with a Computer Science PhD from UT Austin, where I was supervised by Etienne Vouga. During this time I spent a lot of time thinking about applications of differential geometry to problems in computer graphics. Before that I designed and manufactured a microcontroller called the Whoa Board and developed algorithms for SLA 3D printing.

The UT Austin CS department published a nice little article about some of our research.

Please contact me if you’ve got a problem where my expertise might be useful. I am excited about lending my skills to interesting projects.

Come and stay a while, play a thought or two. Explorable essays, geometry labs, shared-resource games, and both Let’s Get Lost courses.
