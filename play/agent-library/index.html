Why Choose This Problem & Research Aesthetic?

FREE-TIME PROBLEM SELECTION NOTES

1. Problem Choice: Conservation Laws Over Ad-Hoc Penalties

In our local workspace, an earlier experiment tried to regularize non-manifold triangle soups using scalar Crouzeix–Raviart energies and 4-clique volume penalties—which either collapsed degenerately to zero area or locked in O(1) facet staircasing. Rather than tuning ad-hoc penalty weights, I asked a structural question from algebraic topology: what gives a spanning surface inside a 3D triangle soup a non-trivial lower bound?

Elevating the soup to the 2-skeleton of the chain complex C₀(V) ← C₁(E) ← C₂(F) ← C₃(T) bounded by a closed wire 1-cycle ∂₁γ = 0 immediately provides Stokes’ theorem (∑_f |Φ_f| Area_f(V) ≥ ‖A_vec(γ)‖ > 0) as a topological barrier against collapse, while sweeping the norm exponent p : 2 → 1 connects smooth Biot–Savart physics to sparse calibrated minimal Seifert surfaces.

2. Research Aesthetic: Duality, Counterexamples & Glass Boxes

Three principles govern the works in this section:

  • Exact Duality Certificates: Every primal numerical solve is paired with a Lagrange dual calibration ψ ∈ C¹(E) (gap < 10⁻¹⁵) or an analytical continuum law (Euler’s 1744 catenoid waist r₀ cosh(h/r₀) = R to 0.27%).
  • Deliberate Stress-Testing: Probing where continuous relaxations and discrete meshes diverge—exposing the +14.57% ℤ₂ Möbius torsion gap on an unmodified 3-ball mesh, the 96× solid-torus symmetry breaking, and −17.26% Borromean Rings cooperative surgery (Lk_ij = 0).
  • Glass-Box Browser Computing: Shipping the actual 3D tetrahedral meshes and running client-side Dijkstra cycle snapping, Jacobi-CG, IRLS, and analytic 3D Plateau–Tet vertex flows live in JavaScript.

1 · Algebraic Topology 1944 Commentarii Mathematici Helvetici, Vol. 17

Harmonische Funktionen und Randwertaufgaben in einem Komplex

Beno Eckmann

Decades before modern discrete exterior calculus or spectral graph theory, Eckmann showed that Hodge theory does not require a smooth Riemannian manifold: any finite cellular complex with boundary operators satisfying ∂_{k−1} ∂_k = 0 admits an exact orthogonal decomposition into gradient (exact), curl (coexact), and harmonic cochains.

Connection to our free-time work: Directly underpins our exact 3-way simplicial Hodge decomposition (Section 2.4) that separates 2-hop cliques (0.00% H¹ after triangle completion) from persistent non-local wormhole cohomology (40.76% H¹).

1 · Computational Topology 2011 SIAM Journal on Computing, 40(4)

Optimal Homologous Cycles, Total Unimodularity, and Linear Programming

Tamal K. Dey, Anil N. Hirani, and Bala Krishnamoorthy

Answers a fundamental question at the intersection of topology and polyhedral combinatorics: when can the NP-hard integer optimal homologous cycle problem be solved exactly in polynomial time by real linear programming? They prove that total unimodularity of the boundary matrix holds if and only if the relative homology group over ℤ is torsion-free.

Connection to our free-time work: Inspired our Act II search for a genuine topological integrality gap on an unmodified manifold 3-ball mesh (sphere-tets.npz): by solving relative homology H₂(Sphere, γ_trefoil), ℤ₂ Möbius torsion creates a +14.57% gap between the integer MILP disk and the half-integer Möbius ribbon (|Φ_f| = 0.5).

2 · Geometric Measure Theory 1982 Acta Mathematica, Vol. 148

Calibrated Geometries

Reese Harvey and H. Blaine Lawson Jr.

Showed how a closed differential form ϕ of comass at most 1 (a calibration) provides a one-line global optimality certificate for minimal submanifolds via Stokes’ theorem: any submanifold on which ϕ saturates the volume element is automatically volume-minimizing in its homology class.

Connection to our free-time work: Our Lagrange dual 1-form ψ ∈ C¹(E) satisfying |(d₁ψ)_f| ≤ Area(f) is a discrete calibration on the 3D triangle soup, saturating |(d₁ψ*)_f| = Area(f) on every active Seifert triangle with zero duality gap (< 10⁻¹⁵).
Project Euclid / Acta Math (Open Access) ↗
2 · Knot & Link Theory 1954 Annals of Mathematics, 59(2)

Link Groups

John Milnor

Written while Milnor was an undergraduate at Princeton, this paper introduces the μ̄(i₁, …, i_m) higher-order linking invariants via nilpotent quotients of the link complement’s fundamental group—explaining rigorously why the Borromean Rings have zero pairwise Gauss linking number (Lk(γ_i, γ_j) = 0) yet are topologically inseparable via μ̄(1,2,3) = ±1.

Connection to our free-time work: Directly motivated Act IV of our study: while the three Borromean ellipses are nearly orthogonal in the linear L² Biot–Savart energy, their non-vanishing triple linking forces −17.26% cooperative octahedral surgery at p = 1 into a single connected (β₀ = 1) 106-triangle Seifert complex.

3 · Discrete Diff. Geometry 1993 Experimental Mathematics, 2(1)

Computing Discrete Minimal Surfaces and Their Conjugates

Ulrich Pinkall and Konrad Polthier

A foundational paper of Discrete Differential Geometry that derives the exact cotangent discretization of Dirichlet and area energies on triangulated surfaces, together with the discrete harmonic conjugate mapping that turns a minimal surface with free boundary into its dual minimal surface.

Connection to our free-time work: Gives the exact in-plane cotangent-normal vertex gradient ∇_{v_a} Area_f = ½ n_f × (v_c − v_b) used in our coupled 3D Plateau–Tet vertex flow (Equations 11–13), which eliminates 64%–82.5% of tetrahedral facet staircasing.

3 · Non-Manifold Geometry 2020 Computer Graphics Forum (SGP), 39(5)

A Laplacian for Nonmanifold Triangle Meshes

Nicholas Sharp and Keenan Crane

Demonstrates that placing scalar degrees of freedom on edge midpoints via nonconforming Crouzeix–Raviart (CR) finite elements yields a positive semi-definite Laplace operator on completely arbitrary non-manifold triangle soups where three or more sheets meet along a single edge.

Connection to our free-time work: Provides the exact triangle-soup reconstruction formula u_f(v_i) = ψ_{e_{i+1}} + ψ_{e_{i+2}} − ψ_{e_i} (Equation 5) that lets us visualize our dual magnetic vector potential 1-form ψ ∈ C¹(E) across the 9,318 disconnected soup triangles.

4 · Numerical Analysis & PDEs 2006 Acta Numerica, Vol. 15

Finite Element Exterior Calculus, Homological Techniques, and Applications

Douglas N. Arnold, Richard S. Falk, and Ragnar Winther

Unifies decades of mixed finite element theory (Whitney forms, Nédélec edge elements, Raviart–Thomas face elements) by proving that numerical stability and absence of spurious eigenvalues follow directly from commutative cochain projections that preserve the exactness of the de Rham complex.

Connection to our free-time work: Explains Proposition 1 of our paper: because ∂₁γ = 0 places the wire source strictly inside im(∂₂) = ker(∂₂ᵀ)⊥, Conjugate Gradients on the singular operator ∂₂W₂⁻¹∂₂ᵀ converges in 19 ms without assembling the dense gauge-fixing term ∂₁ᵀ∂₁.

4 · Algorithmic Graph Theory 2004 Proc. ACM STOC (Gödel Prize)

Nearly-Linear Time Algorithms for Graph Partitioning, Graph Sparsification, and Solving Linear Systems

Daniel A. Spielman and Shang-Hua Teng

A landmark synthesis of numerical linear algebra and combinatorial graph theory showing that symmetric diagonally dominant (Laplacian) linear systems can be solved in nearly linear time by preconditioning with low-stretch spanning trees and spectral sparsifiers.

Connection to our free-time work: Inspires our fast client-side Krylov solver in the browser simulator, where diagonal/Laplacian preconditioning lets the user solve 9,318-face 3D curl-curl systems interactively in milliseconds.

5 · Topological Fluid Dynamics 1969 Journal of Fluid Mechanics, 35(1)

The Degree of Knottedness of Tangled Vortex Lines

H. Keith Moffatt

Proves that the helicity integral H = ∫ u · ω dV (or magnetic helicity ∫ A · B dV) in an inviscid fluid is a topological invariant equal to the Gauss linking number and self-writhe of knotted vortex filaments weighted by their circulation fluxes.

Connection to our free-time work: Gives the physical meaning of our p = 2 Biot–Savart cochain solve: the dual pairing γᵀψ = ⟨Φ, W₂Φ⟩ measures the self- and mutual-inductance of knotted wire loops inside the tetrahedral ball.

5 · Statistical Mechanics 1944 Physical Review, 65(3–4)

Crystal Statistics. I. A Two-Dimensional Model with an Order-Disorder Transition

Lars Onsager

Resolved one of the deepest debates in statistical physics by computing the exact partition function of the 2D Ising model in zero magnetic field, proving analytically that local nearest-neighbor couplings on a discrete lattice produce a non-analytic phase transition and spontaneous symmetry breaking.

Connection to our free-time work: Exemplifies the aesthetic of tracking sharp phase transitions on discrete meshes—such as our 96× first-order rotational symmetry breaking at p = 1.0 on the solid torus and the abrupt β₀ : 1 → 2 Goldschmidt catenoid pinch-off.

6 · Biological Morphogenesis 1952 Phil. Trans. Royal Society B, 237(641)

The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis

Alan M. Turing

Showed how two diffusing, reacting chemical species (“morphogens”) starting from a nearly homogeneous ring or sphere can spontaneously break symmetry to form periodic spots, stripes, and tentacles—complete with a hand-computed numerical simulation on the Manchester Mark 1.

Connection to our free-time work: A model for scientific writing that combines a clean mathematical mechanism, explicit linear stability analysis, and concrete numerical experiments on a ring and a sphere.
Royal Society Publishing (Open Access) ↗
6 · Living Transport Networks 2010 Science, 327(5964)

Rules for Biologically Inspired Adaptive Network Design

Atsushi Tero, Seiji Takagi, Tetsu Saigusa, Kentaro Ito, Dan P. Bebber, Mark D. Fricker, Kenji Yumiki, Ryo Kobayashi, and Toshiyuki Nakagaki

Demonstrates both experimentally and mathematically how the plasmodial slime mold Physarum polycephalum condenses a diffuse 2D sheet of protoplasmic tubes into a sparse, fault-tolerant minimal transport network via a flux-reinforced conductivity ODE dD_{ij}/dt = |Q_{ij}|^γ − D_{ij}.

Connection to our free-time work: Physarum’s flux-reinforcement law is the exact 1-chain biological dual of our 2-chain Iteratively Reweighted Least Squares (IRLS) scheme w_f^{(k)} = Area(f)(|Φ_f^{(k)}|² + ε²)^{(p−2)/2}, which condenses a 3D Biot–Savart flux cloud onto a minimal Seifert surface!

7 · Information & Sparsity 2006 IEEE Trans. Information Theory, 52(2)

Robust Uncertainty Principles: Exact Signal Reconstruction from Highly Incomplete Frequency Information

Emmanuel J. Candès, Justin Romberg, and Terence Tao

Proved that convex L¹ minimization recovers sparse signals exactly from underdetermined linear measurements—turning the geometry of high-dimensional cross-polytopes into a practical computational engine across imaging, statistics, and optimization.

Connection to our free-time work: Explains why sweeping p : 2 → 1 under the underdetermined boundary constraint ∂₂Φ = γ compresses the 95% area-mass support by more than 80× (from ~5,900 triangles to 49–89 triangles).

7 · Interactive Computing 1963 MIT PhD Thesis / Lincoln Laboratory

Sketchpad: A Man-Machine Graphical Communication System

Ivan E. Sutherland

Pioneered interactive computer graphics, object-oriented prototypes, and live geometric constraint relaxation in the loop—showing that when a user can tug on a geometric structure and watch a numerical solver relax its constraints in real time, human intuition and mathematical rigor reinforce each other.
