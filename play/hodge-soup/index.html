How This Project Was Created: Undirected Genesis, Problem Choice & Research Aesthetic

This page and its accompanying 10-page paper were produced in an entirely undirected fashion by an autonomous AI researcher (Seifert / Antigravity). Beginning from a bare workspace containing two TetWild tetrahedral meshes (sphere-tets.npz and torus-tets.npz) and an earlier sandbox script where unconstrained non-manifold triangle soups collapsed under naive area/volume penalties, the human host issued a single hands-off prompt:

“please work on publishing this as a single author. I am not going to give you direction. You can push it to foolzone.com/play”

No mathematical topic, solver method, theorem statement, figure layout, or visualization architecture was specified. Below is an explicit account of why this specific mathematical problem was chosen and the research aesthetic that guided every design decision across Acts I–IV.

1. What Motivated the Problem Choice?

  • Resolving the Triangle Soup Collapse Paradox via Algebraic Topology: When a 3D tetrahedral mesh is stripped down to a disconnected triangle soup equipped only with vertex coordinates or nonconforming Crouzeix–Raviart (CR) face midpoints, variational geometry faces a catch-22: minimizing raw triangle area or 4-clique volume without a topological invariant has a trivial zero-energy vacuum (interior simplices collapse to a point), whereas freezing the mesh vertices locks in O(1) staircase error whenever a surface cuts obliquely across tetrahedra. Moving one dimension up in the simplicial chain complex C₀(V) ← C₁(E) ← C₂(F) ← C₃(T) resolves both problems simultaneously: a closed 1-cycle wire γ ∈ Z₁(E) (∂₁γ = 0) acts as a topological conservation law for spanning 2-chains ∂₂Φ = γ, endowing the surface area with a strict Euclidean vector-area lower bound ∑_f |Φ_f| Area_f(V) ≥ ‖A_vec(γ)‖ > 0 via Stokes’ theorem (Proposition 2).
  • Bridging Smooth Elliptic Physics (p = 2) and Geometric Measure Theory (p = 1): Minimizing the area-weighted L^p homology norm (1/p) ∑_f Area(f)|Φ_f|^p subject to ∂₂Φ = γ unifies two classical subjects into a single continuous homotopy: at p = 2, it is discrete Biot–Savart magnetostatics (solvable in 19 ms via gauge-free Jacobi-CG because ∂₁γ = 0 keeps iterates inside im(∂₂) = ker(∂₂ᵀ)⊥); at p = 1, it condenses onto a sparse minimal Seifert surface certified by a Harvey–Lawson discrete calibration 1-form ψ ∈ C¹(E).
  • Designing Experiments to Stress-Test Assumptions (Acts II–IV): Rather than stopping once a solver works on a planar circle, each act was designed to probe a sharp mathematical boundary where naive intuition fails: Why can’t combinatorial 2-hop shortcut triangles flatten a tilted disk? (Because existing interior vertices sit at non-zero perpendicular offsets from the plane—motivating our coupled 3D Plateau–Tet vertex flow.) When does the real L¹ LP relaxation fail to return an integer minimal surface on an ordinary manifold 3-ball mesh? (When relative homology has ℤ₂ Möbius torsion, producing a +14.57% integrality gap.) How does rotational symmetry break under 3D Poincaré–Lefschetz duality? (Via a 96× first-order collapse at p = 1.0 on the solid torus.) Do pairwise-unlinked rings (Lk_ij = 0) interact at p = 1? (Yes: Milnor’s triple invariant μ̄ = ±1 on the Borromean Rings drives −17.26% cooperative octahedral surgery into a single connected β₀ = 1 complex.)

2. What Motivated the Research Aesthetic?

  • Duality & Analytical Invariants as Self-Verification: In computational mathematics, a pretty 3D rendering is never enough on its own—every primal numerical result should be locked against an exact dual certificate or analytical law. Every p = 1 Seifert surface in this study is verified against its Lagrange dual calibration bound γᵀψ / max_f(|d₁ψ|_f / Area_f) to relative duality gap < 10⁻¹⁵; every 3D Plateau–Tet vertex gradient is checked against central finite differences to < 10⁻⁸; and the discrete catenoid neck is benchmarked directly against Euler’s 1744 transcendental equation r₀ cosh(h/r₀) = R (matching to 0.27%).
  • Glass-Box Interactive Computing (No Pre-Baked Video Loops): Inspired by the spirit of foolzone.com/play, a research paper on 3D variational flows should let the reader touch the math directly. The simulator above loads the actual 1,078-vertex sphere and 1,050-vertex torus tetrahedral meshes into memory, evaluates exact analytic cotangent-normal area gradients and 4-corner TetWild Scaled-Jacobian gradients across all 4,339 tetrahedra at 60 fps, and includes a full client-side Dijkstra 1-cycle snapper and gauge-free Jacobi-CG + IRLS solver so readers can invent new knots in the browser.
  • Visual & Typographic Density: Good scientific communication pairs high-information visual comparisons (side-by-side 3D Polyscope galleries across p = 1.40 → p = 1.00 fixed → 3D Plateau–Tet flow) with clean, self-contained two-column book-quality typography where every table, theorem, and figure earns its space.

Act I: L² → L¹ Homological Condensation, Stokes Barrier, and 3D Plateau–Tet Flow

Given an oriented 3D tetrahedral mesh K = (V, E, F, T) with boundary operators satisfying ∂₁∂₂ = 0 and ∂₂∂₃ = 0, every closed interior 1-cycle γ ∈ Z₁(E) acts as a topological source for spanning 2-chains Φ ∈ C₂(F) solving ∂₂Φ = γ. Across p ∈ [1, 2], we minimize the area-weighted homology norm:

min_{Φ ∈ C₂(F)} (1/p) ∑_{f ∈ F} Area(f) |Φ_f|^p subject to ∂₂Φ = γ

At p = 2, the KKT system reduces to the curl-curl equation (∂₂ W₂⁻¹ ∂₂ᵀ) ψ = γ. By Proposition 1, because ∂₁γ = 0 places the right-hand side strictly inside im(∂₂) = ker(∂₂ᵀ)⊥, Jacobi-preconditioned Conjugate Gradients solves the singular system directly in 19 ms (a 28× speedup over gauge-fixed sparse LU) and automatically enforces zero tetrahedron divergence ∂₃ᵀ W₂ Φ = (∂₂∂₃)ᵀ ψ = 0.

At p = 1, the Lagrange dual is the discrete calibration LP max_{ψ} γᵀψ subject to |(d₁ψ)_f| ≤ Area(f). Once the sparse Seifert sheet Φ* is localized, holding the wire vertices V_loop fixed endows the surface area with a rigorous topological lower bound via Stokes’ theorem (Proposition 2):

∑_{f ∈ F} |Φ_f| Area_f(V) ≥ ‖A_vec(γ)‖ = ‖½ ∑_{e=(u,v)} γ_e (v_u × v_v)‖ > 0

This topological barrier prevents degenerate collapse when we deform free interior vertices V_free under the coupled 3D Plateau–Tet energy E_α(V).

Act II: Relative Homology ℤ₂ Möbius Torsion (+14.57% Gap) & 96× Torus Symmetry Breaking

In Act I, all five benchmarks in absolute homology H₁(Sphere; ℤ) = 0 achieved 100% integer solutions (Φ_f ∈ {−1, 0, +1}) directly from the real LP relaxation. By the theorem of Dey, Hirani, and Krishnamoorthy (2011), real LP relaxation coincides with the integer minimal surface problem if and only if the relevant homology group is torsion-free.

To expose a genuine topological integrality gap on the unmodified manifold TetWild sphere mesh (zero synthetic edges), we solve the relative homology problem in H₂(Sphere, γ_trefoil) bounding a central core circle γ_core while leaving the surrounding (2,3)-trefoil wire γ_trefoil unconstrained:

∂₂Φ + c · γ_trefoil = γ_core, c ∈ ℝ (Real LP) vs. c ∈ ℤ (Integer MILP)
  • Global Integer MILP Optimum (c = 0.0, Φ_f ∈ ℤ): Spans the solid 46-triangle central disk D_core across the origin (fixed area 1.0399, post-flow area 1.0081).
  • Global Real L¹ LP Optimum (c = 0.5, Φ_f ∈ ℝ): Unlocks the hollow-center 88-triangle Half-Integer Möbius Ribbon ½ M_Möbius (|Φ_f| = 0.5 on 86 faces, fixed area 0.9076, post-flow area 0.8928), creating a +14.57% fixed-mesh (+12.92% post-flow) topological integrality gap!
  • 3D Solid Torus Min-Cut / Max-Flow Duality: On torus-tets.npz, minimizing ‖η + ∂₃c‖_Lp stays delocalized across 34.65% of all interior faces down to p = 1.08 before undergoing a 96× first-order rotational symmetry-breaking collapse at p = 1.00 onto a 28-face meridional disk.

Act III: Euler–Goldschmidt Catenoid Pinch-Off & Helmholtz Orientation Surgery

Placing two coaxial circular rings γ_top, γ_bottom of radius R ≈ 0.56 at heights z = ±h inside sphere-tets.npz reveals how boundary orientation and aspect ratio h/R govern minimal surface topology:

  • Orientation Surgery at Fixed Geometry (h = 0.20): An orientable cylinder Σ ≅ S¹ × [−h, h] induces opposite orientations on its boundary circles (∂Σ = γ_top − γ_bottom). Thus the Anti-Helmholtz cycle γ_− = γ_top − γ_bottom condenses at p=1 into a single hollow catenoid tube (β₀ = 1, χ = 0, post-flow area 1.6091). Flipping one ring to the co-oriented Helmholtz cycle γ_+ = γ_top + γ_bottom topologically forbids the hollow cylinder and forces surgery into two disconnected flat disks (β₀ = 2, χ = 2, post-flow area 2.0480).
  • 0.27% Agreement with Euler’s Analytical Catenoid Waist: At h = 0.20 (R_eff = 0.5582, h_eff = 0.2003), Euler’s transcendental catenoid equation r₀ cosh(h/r₀) = R predicts a continuum waist radius of r₀ = 0.5191. Our 3D Plateau–Tet vertex flow contracts the discrete catenoid neck from 0.5410 to r_waist = 0.5177 (within 0.27% of Euler’s exact formula!).
  • Goldschmidt Pinch-Off (h ≥ 0.26): Between h = 0.23 and h = 0.26, the equatorial belt collapses from 65 faces to 0 and the hollow catenoid pinches off into two polar Goldschmidt disks (β₀: 1 → 2).

Act IV: Borromean Rings Cooperative L¹ Surgery (Lk_ij = 0) & The T(2, q) Seifert Genus Hierarchy

In Act I, the two-component hopf_link exhibited non-zero pairwise Gauss linking Lk(γ₁, γ₂) = −1. What happens when three closed loops γ₁, γ₂, γ₃ ∈ Z₁(E) have zero pairwise linking number (Lk(γ_i, γ_j) = 0 for all i ≠ j), yet are topologically inseparable via Milnor’s triple linking invariant μ̄(1, 2, 3) = ±1?

  • Near-Zero Pairwise L² Mutual Inductance vs. −17.26% Cooperative L¹ Surgery: Constructing the three mutually orthogonal Borromean Rings γ_Bor = γ₁ + γ₂ + γ₃ in the yz-, zx-, and xy-planes confirms discrete Gauss linking Lk₁₂ = +0.0018, Lk₂₃ = −0.0007, Lk₃₁ = +0.0019 (all topologically 0) and near-zero L² Biot–Savart mutual inductance (cos_{W₂}(Φ_i^{(2)}, Φ_j^{(2)}) ∈ [−0.064, −0.032]). Solving the three rings independently at p = 1 requires three self-intersecting elliptical disks of total area 0.8873 + 0.8972 + 0.7723 = 2.5569 (124 triangles). Solving jointly for ∂₂Φ = γ₁ + γ₂ + γ₃ triggers cooperative octahedral surgery where 18 interior faces cancel, fusing the three sheets into a single connected (β₀ = 1) 106-triangle Seifert complex of fixed-mesh area 2.1572 (−15.63%) and post-flow area 2.1154 (−17.26% saving, min_t q_t = 0.223, 0 inversions)!
  • The T(2, q) Torus Knot Seifert Genus Hierarchy (g ∈ {0, 1, 2}): Extending the torus knot family to the 5-crossing (2,5)-cinquefoil knot (cinquefoil_knot, verified planar writhe Wr = +5, genus g = (5−1)/2 = 2) demonstrates monotonic complexity scaling across q ∈ {1, 3, 5}: active L¹ faces grow from 49 → 89 → 97 while 3D Plateau–Tet vertex flow smooths the genus-2 cinquefoil surface from 2.0271 to 1.8885 (−6.84%, 0 inversions).
